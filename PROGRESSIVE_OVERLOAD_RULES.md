# 📈 Progressive Overload Rules - Week 1 to Week 2

## Overview
Progressive overload ensures continuous adaptation and improvement. Week 2 systematically increases training stimulus while respecting time constraints and recovery capacity.

---

## 🏋️ Strength Training

### Main Work Sets (4-6 reps, 6-8 reps, 8-12 reps)
**Rule:** +2 reps every week

**Examples:**
```
Week 1: Goblet Squat 4×6 @ 55kg
Week 2: Goblet Squat 4×8 @ 55kg (+2 reps)

Week 1: Bent Over Row 4×10 @ 32kg
Week 2: Bent Over Row 4×12 @ 32kg (+2 reps)

Week 1: Leg Press 3×12 @ 68kg
Week 2: Leg Press 3×14 @ 68kg (+2 reps)
```

**Rationale:** 
- Systematic volume increase
- Same weight, more reps = progressive overload
- Prepares for weight increase in Block 2

---

### Warm-up Sets
**Rule:** NO PROGRESSION - Keep at 10 reps

**Examples:**
```
Week 1: Goblet Squat 2×10 @ 18kg (warm-up)
Week 2: Goblet Squat 2×10 @ 18kg (SAME - no progression)

Week 1: Resistance Band Pull-Apart 2×15
Week 2: Resistance Band Pull-Apart 2×15 (SAME)
```

**Rationale:**
- Warm-ups are for activation and joint mobilization, not progression
- 10 reps is optimal for muscle activation before heavy sets
- Saves time and prevents boredom
- Consistent warm-up = better performance tracking on main sets

---

### Timed Exercises (Plank, Holds)
**Rule:** +15 seconds per week, capped at 2 minutes (120 seconds)

**Examples:**
```
Week 1: Plank 45 sec
Week 2: Plank 60 sec (+15 sec)

Week 1: Plank 60 sec
Week 2: Plank 75 sec (+15 sec)

Week 1: Plank 105 sec
Week 2: Plank 120 sec (+15 sec, capped at 2 min)

Week 1: Plank 120 sec
Week 2: Plank 120 sec (NO PROGRESSION - already at cap)
```

**Rationale:**
- 15 seconds = nice round number (60 sec looks better than 55 sec)
- More motivating to hit 1 min, 1:15, 1:30, etc.
- 2 min cap = once you can hold 2 min plank, you're strong enough
- Beyond 2 min = diminishing returns, better to add weight or progress to harder variation

## 🏃 Running

### Intervals
**Rule:** SAME rounds, but RPE-based progression

**Examples:**
```
Week 1: 6×500m @ RPE 8/10
Week 2: 6×500m @ RPE 8/10 (SAME rounds, but naturally faster)

Week 1: 8×400m @ Race pace
Week 2: 8×400m @ Race pace (SAME rounds)
```

**Rationale:**
- Time constraints: clients may not have time for +2 intervals
- RPE 8/10 in Week 2 will naturally be faster than Week 1 due to adaptation
- Progression comes from improved pace at same effort level
- Prevents overtraining and respects recovery capacity
- Focus on consistency and pacing, not volume increase

**Notes Added to Intervals:**
> "Target RPE 8/10 (hard effort). Week 2: Same rounds, but your 8/10 effort will naturally be faster as you adapt. Focus on consistent pacing across all intervals."

---

### Long Runs & Tempo Runs
**Rule:** +1-2km distance OR +5-10 min duration

**Examples:**
```
Week 1: Long Run 8km @ 60 min
Week 2: Long Run 9km @ 65 min (+1km, +5 min)

Week 1: Tempo Run 5km @ 25 min
Week 2: Tempo Run 6km @ 30 min (+1km, +5 min)
```

**Rationale:**
- Distance/duration progression for aerobic base building
- Intervals stay same to respect time constraints
- Long runs can be extended as they're typically on weekends

---

## 🔄 Cardio & Conditioning

### Erg Workouts (Rower, SkiErg, Bike)
**Rule:** +1-2 rounds OR +100-200m distance

**Examples:**
```
Week 1: 5 rounds × 250m row
Week 2: 6 rounds × 250m row (+1 round)

Week 1: 2000m row for time
Week 2: 2200m row for time (+200m)
```

---

## 🧘 Mobility & Recovery

### Post-Workout Mobility
**Rule:** NO PROGRESSION - Same duration

**Examples:**
```
Week 1: Cat-Cow 60s
Week 2: Cat-Cow 60s (SAME)

Week 1: Hip Flexor Stretch 45s each side
Week 2: Hip Flexor Stretch 45s each side (SAME)
```

**Rationale:**
- Mobility is for recovery, not progression
- Consistency is key for flexibility gains
- Time-capped at 3-4 min total

---

### Active Recovery
**Rule:** NO PROGRESSION - Same exercises and duration

**Rationale:**
- Rest day = recovery, not progression
- Focus on movement quality, not intensity

## 🎯 Key Principles

1. **Systematic:** Clear, predictable progression rules
2. **Sustainable:** Respects time constraints and recovery
3. **Motivating:** Round numbers (60 sec, not 55 sec)
4. **Smart:** Warm-ups don't progress (saves time/energy)
5. **RPE-based:** Running intervals progress via effort, not volume
6. **Capped:** Plank maxes at 2 min (diminishing returns)
7. **Recovery-focused:** Mobility doesn't progress (it's for recovery)

## 🔄 Future Blocks

**Block 2 (Weeks 3-4):**
- Increase weight by 5-10% (now that reps have increased)
- Reset reps to starting range (e.g., 4×8 → 4×6 @ heavier weight)
- Continue RPE-based running progression

**Block 3 (Weeks 5-6):**
- Continue weight progression
- Introduce new exercise variations
- Maintain RPE-based approach for cardio
