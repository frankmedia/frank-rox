# Programme Structure Explanation

## How Exercise Numbers Are Determined

The programme builder creates workouts based on your **onboarding answers** and **focus areas**. Here's how it works:

### Training Day Structure

Each training day can have **multiple sessions**:

1. **Main Workout Session** (Strength, Running, or Cardio)
   - This is the primary workout for the day
   - Number of exercises depends on the workout type:
     - **Strength Lower**: 4-5 exercises (Squat, Split Squat, RDL, Leg Press, Core)
     - **Strength Upper**: 5-6 exercises (Bench, Row, Shoulder Press, Lat Pulldown, Accessories, Core)
     - **Running Intervals**: 1-3 exercises (Warm-up run, Intervals, Cool-down)
     - **Long Run**: 1 exercise (Single long run)

2. **Post-Workout Mobility** (Added to EVERY training day)
   - Always 3 exercises: Cat-Cow, Hip Flexor Stretch, Glute Bridge
   - Takes 10-15 minutes
   - Helps with recovery

### Example Day Breakdown

**Day 1: Strength Lower + Mobility**
- Main Strength Session: 5 exercises
  1. Dumbbell Goblet Squat (4×6)
  2. Rear-Foot Elevated Split Squat (3×10)
  3. DB Romanian Deadlift (3×10)
  4. Leg Press (3×12)
  5. Plank (45 sec)
- Post-Workout Mobility: 3 exercises
  6. Cat-Cow (1 min)
  7. Hip Flexor Stretch (1 min)
  8. Glute Bridge (1 min)

**Total: 8 exercises** (5 strength + 3 mobility)

**Day 2: Running Intervals + Mobility**
- Main Running Session: 3 exercises
  1. Warm-up Run (5 min)
  2. Intervals (6×1km)
  3. Cool-down Run (10 min)
- Post-Workout Mobility: 3 exercises
  4. Cat-Cow (1 min)
  5. Hip Flexor Stretch (1 min)
  6. Glute Bridge (1 min)

**Total: 6 exercises** (3 running + 3 mobility)

**Day 7: Active Recovery (Rest Day)**
- Full Recovery Session: 11 exercises
  1. Inchworms (2 min)
  2. Thoracic Rotation (2 min)
  3. Standing Hip CARs (2 min)
  4. 90/90 Hip Switches (2 min)
  5. Hamstring Stretch (2 min)
  6. Quad Stretch (2 min)
  7. Cossack Squat (2 min)
  8. Figure of 4 Stretch (2 min)
  9. Bird Dog (2 min)
  10. Dead Bug (2 min)
  11. Plank (1 min)

**Total: 11 exercises** (all mobility/recovery)

### Why Different Days Have Different Exercise Counts

1. **Strength days** have more exercises because they target multiple muscle groups
2. **Running days** have fewer exercises because the intervals are the main focus
3. **Recovery days** have many short mobility exercises (1-2 min each)
4. **Every training day** gets 3 mobility exercises added automatically

### Focus Areas Impact

Your focus areas (Running, Strength, Cardio) determine:
- How many strength days you get (1-3 per week)
- How many running sessions (0-5 per week)
- Whether cardio/conditioning is included

If you selected:
- **Strength focus**: You'll get 2-3 strength days with 8-9 exercises each
- **Running focus**: You'll get 2-5 running days with 4-6 exercises each
- **Both**: You'll get a mix, with some days having fewer exercises

This is **by design** - quality over quantity. Each workout is tailored to your goals!
